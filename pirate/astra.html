Description Radio Astra, formerly known as Radio ROJX AM, is a pirate station that was originally broadcasted from France and is now paused and in the future on Radio Channel292 on real Shortwave. The station has been known for its unique and experimental content, which includes music and experimental programming. The station's slogan is "The Real Shortwave Station"
The station is located in France, but it is expected to be broadcasted from Germany in the future via Radio Channel292.
