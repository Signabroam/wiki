Description ASYP is a CW station that is known to be associated with Pikhta26. It operates on various frequencies throughout the year, with specific shifts in UTC time. The station has been active for several years and is known for monolith transmissions in CW mode. It is located in the Eastern Far District of Russia and has several known counterpart stations, including S5826, S9062, and S12035.
